Client Case Study · Passive Workforce

A repeatable acquisition system for a healthcare agency coaching business.

Passive Workforce teaches new and established home care agency owners how to build and scale their businesses. We built the webinar funnels, the ad creative, and a GoHighLevel CRM that sorts and follows up with every lead automatically.

$20K+/mo
Added Monthly Revenue
2–3x
Return on Ad Spend
The Challenge

A strong offer with no system to scale it.

Passive Workforce had a proven program and real authority in the home care space, but growth depended heavily on organic content. Revenue went up and down month to month, with no reliable way to put the offer in front of the right agency owners and no system to capture, qualify, and follow up with the leads that came in.

They could deliver strong results for their clients, but couldn't predict where next month's enrollments would come from.

What We Built

What we built.

We built the whole system, from the ad creative to the automations that follow up with every lead, and we track every piece of it.

01

Webinar Funnel

Long-form and short-form registration landing pages, a confirmation page with FAQ videos, and a webinar built to convert agency owners into booked calls.

02

Meta Ad Campaigns

Direct-response talking-head and static ad creative, with messaging segmented for both newly licensed and established home care agency owners.

03

GoHighLevel CRM

A full pipeline build with lead-sorting automations, custom fields, conditional qualification logic, and email and SMS nurture sequences.

04

Tracking & Reporting

Meta Pixel tracking, custom conversions, and live Looker Studio dashboards, so performance was visible as it happened.

The Approach

Ads built from real sales calls.

We went through real sales call transcripts to see what separated agency owners who bought from those who didn't. That shaped the ad scripts, the landing page copy, and the qualification logic in the CRM.

The ads and funnel videos featured the program's expert alongside Veronika Oquendo as presenter, so the same faces showed up from the first ad through the funnel. Every asset was built for mobile and tested before launch.

“He's built a whole system for us that actually works and has been delivering consistently for months. That kind of consistency is huge — so much goes into the prep, the ads, and the follow-up.”

Veronika Oquendo Passive Workforce · Healthcare Education
The Results
  • $20K+ in added monthly revenue driven through the Meta-powered webinar funnel.
  • Consistent results month over month, so enrollments no longer rose and fell with organic content.
  • Every lead automatically sorted, qualified, and followed up with through the GoHighLevel build.
