Webinar Funnels

How we build and run your webinar funnel.

Most of our work today is webinar and live event funnels. Here's every piece we build, in the order a registrant moves through it, from the first ad to a booked sales call.

01

The ads

15 to 30 short talking-head videos you film from scripts we write, plus 10 to 15 static image ads. Each one speaks to a specific problem your buyers have. We run them on Meta and keep testing new variations as the data comes in.

02

Registration page

A short page with qualifying questions you choose, like role, budget or timeline. Only answers that match your buyers get reported back to Meta as a lead, so the ads learn to find more people like them.

03

Thank-you page

4 to 8 short videos, each one answering an objection that comes up on your sales calls, so people arrive at the webinar already warmed up.

04

Reminders

Email and text reminders between registration and the live session, built to get registrants into the room.

05

The live webinar

Slides and script written with you, built from what you already teach and how you already sell. You present live, usually every two weeks or once a month. We set up the webinar tech.

06

Follow-up

Three separate tracks after the webinar: people who saw the offer, people who left before it, and no-shows. Plus a replay page. Every track points to a booked call.

07

Tracking and reporting

Every registrant tagged by ad, source and whether they attended, with a dashboard showing spend, registrations, show rate and booked calls.
