Before We Talk

Come with your numbers.

The financial model gets built from what you know about your own business. The more of this you have handy, the more specific the call gets.

  • What your offer sells for, and how clients find you today.
  • Any past ads or webinar numbers, even rough ones. Failed campaigns are useful data.
  • Your current LTV and CAC: how long a client stays and what they're worth over that time, plus your current cost to acquire a customer if you're currently spending on ads.
  • Your list and your tools: email list size, any groups or audiences you've built, and what you run on today.

Don't have the numbers? Come anyway. Untracked metrics are often the first leak the call finds.
