Ads Work for Home Services.
The Problem Is What Happens After the Click.

Most home service businesses grow through referrals, partnerships, and door-to-door. The sales process for cold leads from ads is entirely different.

TALK: "So here's what I've found after running my own home service business that was almost entirely reliant on ads and running ads for dozens of other businesses. The ad itself is rarely the problem. Ads do work for home services. The part that almost always breaks is everything that happens after a lead clicks an ad. Most home service businesses start and scale through strategies like referrals, partnerships and door-to-door sales teams. What this means is that most of the leads you're getting in touch with have some kind of warm touch point first. But the sales process for leads coming from ads is entirely different. There needs to be more trust built into the sales process, they need to have their questions answered before you reach out and they need to know why they should go with you instead of your competitors. I know this because I've gone through the growing pains of running ads for my own business and I got tired of calling leads who didn't know who we were, didn't know why they submitted a form and had a low likelihood of ever actually booking a job."

3 Things That Actually Move the Needle

  • Properly qualifying leads on the ad so you attract people who actually want to book
  • Backend systems that warm leads up on your business before you ever call them
  • A dedicated sales process for cold leads, because they're different from referrals
TALK: "So after going through this with my own business and working with other businesses in the industry, there's a couple strategies we've found that make a huge difference. These are all strategies we've taken and refined from our own business or taken from running ads in other industries. Number one is properly qualifying leads on the ad. Most agencies are incentivized to give you a bunch of leads so they optimize for volume and leave you with crappy leads. But instead by using the proper qualifying language on the ad and having systems in place post click, you can attract leads that are actively searching for your services and wanting to book a job. Number two, backend sales systems. This is something I took from other industries I was running ads for. So before a lead is ever getting a call from your business, the goal is that they've consumed a bunch of information on your business, the service you offer and why they should choose you. This essentially warms them up before you reach out and builds the trust that a referral from their neighbor would. Number three is a dedicated sales process for cold leads. As I alluded to earlier, cold leads have different needs than a lead that came from a referral or partnership. So we developed call scripts, email templates and all those kinds of things that warm up your business quickly before you ever reach out."
